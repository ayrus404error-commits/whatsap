# Privacy

Whatsap is a client app. It has **no servers, accounts, analytics, ads or
tracking** of its own.

- **Who it talks to:** only WhatsApp's servers, directly from your phone, using
  WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted multi-device protocol (Signal protocol, via
  whatsmeow). It also fetches link previews from the website a link points to
  when you send that link.
- **What it stores:** your messages, contacts' names, media you download and the
  device's encryption keys, in the app's private storage on your phone. Media you
  save goes to your Pictures, Movies or Downloads folder. Uninstalling the app or
  clearing its data removes everything it stored. Unlink it from *WhatsApp →
  Linked devices* to end the session.
- **Permissions:** camera and microphone (photos, video notes, voice notes),
  photos (sending media, the "Send photo?" screenshot suggestion), contacts
  (showing the name you saved after you add someone), notifications, and running
  in the background (receiving messages). Each permission is asked for when a
  feature first needs it, and you can refuse it.
- **Crash reports** are never sent automatically. If the app crashes, it offers to
  open your email app with the report, and you decide whether to send it.

WhatsApp itself still processes your account as described in WhatsApp's own
privacy policy.
